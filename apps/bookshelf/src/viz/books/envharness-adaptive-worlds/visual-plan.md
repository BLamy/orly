# Awakening Static Worlds — visual plan

Paper: **EnvHarness: Awakening Static Worlds for Agent Learning**
Stable ID: **arXiv:2608.19880**
Official code snapshot: `google-research/envharness` at `fab7d57441f06b75c73a900e04561d4d7600f361`

Throughline: **the lesson**. A benchmark begins as a frozen lesson. EnvHarness reshapes the interface around that lesson, EnvRigger tunes it to the learner, and the final chapter measures what the learner carries back to untouched held-out tasks.

## Chapter 1 — A membrane around the world

Visual idea: a frozen benchmark sits inside an orbital interface membrane. Action and observation particles circulate through `reset`, `step`, `observe`, and `evaluate`; the membrane thickens into an EnvHarness without disturbing the verifier beam at the core.

Grounding: `envharness/core/actionable_env.py`, `envharness/core/envharness.py`, paper Sections 1 and 2.1.

Beats:

1. A static benchmark repeats the same lesson regardless of the learner.
2. The wrapper appears around the world instead of rebuilding it.
3. The five real `ActionableEnv` methods light around the membrane.
4. Action enters through `step`; observation comes back through the same boundary.
5. The wrapper itself satisfies `ActionableEnv`, so layers stack without changing the policy-facing contract.
6. The transition backend remains frozen while the wrapper reshapes interface traffic.
7. `evaluate` still reaches the original verifier; success is not generated by the wrapper.
8. Five benchmark domains orbit the same interface.
9. The final equation lands on a clean panel: customized environment equals static environment plus EnvHarness.

## Chapter 2 — Pre-roll and three gates

Visual idea: one persistent episode tape runs left to right. `Setup` winds the tape before the policy head starts; then the same tape passes through the action, transition, and observation gates implemented by `Rules`.

Grounding: `envharness/harnesses/setup.py`, `envharness/harnesses/rules.py`, `envharness/core/code_loader.py`, paper Section 2.2 and Appendix A (`Stage → Setups`, `Contract → Rules`).

Beats:

1. The paper's Stage appears under its released-code name, `Setup`.
2. A deterministic action list replays after `inner.reset` and before the policy sees its first observation.
3. The tape lands at a different starting state while the underlying task stays the same.
4. The paper's Contract becomes a generated `_Rules(Rules)` subclass.
5. The action gate can pass, rewrite, or return `Blocked` while preserving the current observation.
6. The transition gate sees the post-step state; the observation gate filters what the policy receives.
7. Reward and the terminal verifier remain untouched by `Rules`.
8. `load_rules_subclass` compiles the emitted code and requires a top-level `_Rules` subclass.
9. The clean ending shows the three axes acting on one tape, not three replacement environments.

## Chapter 3 — Stitching a longer horizon

Visual idea: two independent episode reels are spliced into one longer film. The splice advances from A to B only after A finishes, carries a bounded tail of context, and closes under a two-key AND lock.

Grounding: `envharness/harnesses/link.py`, `tests/test_link_envagnostic.py`, paper Section 2.2 and Table 5.

Beats:

1. Short tasks can teach an agent to stop as soon as the first success appears.
2. `Link` holds any two `ActionableEnv` implementations without inspecting their internal shapes.
3. Only environment A resets at the beginning; B is lazy-reset at handoff.
4. A's steps fill the first reel while Link masks its termination as the composite episode's end.
5. At the handoff, A is evaluated and B's first observation may receive a bounded context splice.
6. B continues under the same policy-facing episode.
7. Final success is `a.evaluate().success AND b.evaluate().success`.
8. The paper's Chain result appears: combined Stage/Contract plus Chain skills reached 54.30 success rate and 43.12 average steps.
9. The final frame leaves one long goal tape with both verifier keys lit.

## Chapter 4 — The diagnostic lathe

Visual idea: five baseline trajectories form a fingerprint on a rotating diagnostic wheel. A diagnosis is cut into candidate code, launched into five isolated rollout capsules, and routed through ACCEPT, REFINE, or REJECT before another turn of the wheel.

Grounding: `envharness/orchestration/orchestrator.py`, `envharness/orchestration/runner.py`, `envharness/agents/harness_agent.py`, `envharness/core/code_loader.py`, paper Section 3.2 and Table 8.

Beats:

1. EnvRigger treats the policy as a black box and starts with five baseline rollouts.
2. Successes and failures form a capability fingerprint rather than a single verdict.
3. Diagnose finds a concrete behavior such as an action loop or unread observation.
4. `HarnessAgent.propose` emits `rules_code` and optional `in_env_actions`; it writes the layer rather than choosing a fixed schema.
5. The runner builds `Rules(Setup(base))` and launches each episode in a fresh subprocess.
6. Malformed generated code becomes an error trace before expensive bridge startup; one bad candidate does not kill the loop.
7. Five fresh candidate rollouts are judged in aggregate.
8. ACCEPT persists the candidate; REFINE returns traces to writing; REJECT asks for a new proposal.
9. The write-and-validate loop has a five-round revision budget and then hands accepted lessons forward.

## Chapter 5 — The lesson moves with the learner

Visual idea: one learner marker advances along a capability frontier while benchmark bars and a scaling curve transform around it. Each accepted environment moves the frontier, so the next round targets a newly exposed weakness.

Grounding: paper Tables 2–5 and Figure 5; `README.md` results tables; `experiments/*/reasoning_bank_eval.py`.

Beats:

1. Evaluation returns to untouched held-out tasks; the harness is a training signal, not a changed test.
2. ALFWorld average moves from 62.4 to 68.3, including a 9.0-point out-of-distribution gain.
3. WebArena average moves from 38.5 to 41.6.
4. SWE-bench Verified success rises from 49.88 to 52.58 while average steps fall from 55.01 to 49.61.
5. OfficeQA exact match rises from 54.40 to 56.20; SpreadsheetBench pass at one rises from 45.88 to 49.15.
6. In reinforcement learning, ALFWorld in-distribution success moves from 81.4 to 87.9.
7. With 300 environments, the co-evolving curve reaches 54.79 versus 52.13 for original and 50.37 for generated environments.
8. The final recap retraces frozen world → interface layer → generated component → fresh rollouts → carried skill.
9. The quiet ending states the mechanism: reshape the lesson at the learner's current boundary, then keep moving the boundary.
