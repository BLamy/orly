# Replay QA: { fully managed }
*End-to-end testing that explores, runs and records on its own*

> You give Replay QA one URL. It explores your app, turns what it finds into journeys and runs them on a schedule, against staging, a preview or localhost, and on every pull request, recording every run. So when a bug only exists in time (a 67 ms commit nobody clicked, one list fetched 258 times), it arrives with a recording and its root cause.
