# Treatment C — "Two Ropes" (two lineages braided into one commit)

**Logline.** Two lines of work, one inside Cloudflare and one at Deno, solved the same problem in opposite
places: one built a global storage relay you could only rent; the other built a single binary that needs only a
bucket. On 28 October 2026 the second began to be merged into the first's open runtime.

**Spine.** Rope one (Kenton Varda's posts): 2020 one object, one place in the world; 2021 the output gate holds
the reply; 2022 the runtime is opened; 2024 SQLite in the same thread and the Storage Relay Service (five
followers, three to confirm, batches to object storage). Rope two (Ryan Dahl): celld, August 2026, one binary
in Rust, object storage as its only dependency; its replicator descended from Litestream. The braid: 9 October
2026, the team joins; 28 October, the commit. Then a long technical third act unbraids the commit's mechanisms.

**Strengths.** Concrete attribution; respects both teams equally; makes the "making it more open" request the
emotional arc; SRS is a lovely ancestor for celld's followers.

**Weaknesses.** Biography-heavy; mechanisms arrive late; risks implying motives the posts do not state; the
"acquisition" framing must stay to what was announced.

**Graft value.** The two-rope braid as the 2026 history image; SRS as the hosted ancestor of celld's fleet
(clearly labelled as a different system with different numbers); the Litestream lineage on the sources card.
