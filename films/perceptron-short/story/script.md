### ch01-s03 · Inside the machine's mind: a lantern-lit theatre of photocells · 1950s · candleAmber · in: iris

_The perceptron retina teaching machine (captioned as an illustration of the rule): a 20 by 20 grid of photocell lanterns shows a shape; threads of light run through a short tangle to dials and on to the response unit's needle, which swings with the weighted total and answers by whether it passes a threshold mark.
