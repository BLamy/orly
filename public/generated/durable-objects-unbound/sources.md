# Sources — Durable Objects, Unbound

Research as of **October 31, 2026**. The film distinguishes the supplied initial workerd cluster commit from the later inspected implementation. Illustrative room names, messages, page numbers, and version tokens are teaching fixtures.

- [Durable Objects closed-beta introduction — September 28, 2020](https://blog.cloudflare.com/introducing-workers-durable-objects): identity, coordination, private state, and chat-room model.
- [Open-source workerd beta — September 27, 2022](https://blog.cloudflare.com/workerd-open-source-workers-runtime/): the runtime was already open source; the 2026 change concerns practical distributed self-hosting.
- [SQLite in Durable Objects — September 26, 2024](https://blog.cloudflare.com/sqlite-in-durable-objects/): local embedded SQL and the distinction between local query work and distributed durability.
- [Cloudflare announcement — October 9, 2026](https://blog.cloudflare.com/deno-joins-cloudflare/) and [Deno announcement](https://deno.com/blog/cloudflare): the teams’ stated direction and announced transition plans. Business motivations are attributed to their authors; no acquisition price is asserted.
- [Supplied workerd commit `aa494794c44a900592c8281b191302d3b0a6e2a5`](https://github.com/cloudflare/workerd/commit/aa494794c44a900592c8281b191302d3b0a6e2a5): Ryan Dahl’s initial shared-bucket clustering change, October 29 UTC (October 28 Pacific). Experimental configuration, ownership, forwarding, WAL capture, bucket durability, takeover; no alarms at that snapshot.
- [Inspected workerd snapshot `ad78a8fe76b32dd4f19c58d81b2e03e10a996bba` — October 31, 2026](https://github.com/cloudflare/workerd/tree/ad78a8fe76b32dd4f19c58d81b2e03e10a996bba/src/workerd/server/cluster): `ownership.rs`, `registry.rs`, `logic/lease.rs`, `capture.rs`, `owner.rs`, `log.rs`, and `alarms.rs`. [Integration tests](https://github.com/cloudflare/workerd/blob/ad78a8fe76b32dd4f19c58d81b2e03e10a996bba/src/workerd/server/tests/cluster/cluster-test.mjs) include single-writer behavior, forwarding, killed-owner recovery, alarms on a survivor, and facets. Source/test inspection is not a claim that this film executed upstream tests.
- [celld v0.6.3 snapshot `8702f6d186c77698dbfb30cf29e3b1922a88f008` — October 21, 2026](https://github.com/denoland/celld/tree/8702f6d186c77698dbfb30cf29e3b1922a88f008), especially [guarantees](https://github.com/denoland/celld/blob/8702f6d186c77698dbfb30cf29e3b1922a88f008/docs/guarantees.md), `crates/celld/ownership_store.rs`, `ltx_repl.rs`, `node_log.rs`, `crates/logic/output_gate.rs`, and `crates/ltx/src/db.rs`: bucket assumptions, epoch fencing, WAL-to-LTX capture, fleet proofs and sealed-tail recovery. This later celld mechanism must not be attributed wholesale to workerd’s initial merge.
- [celld project](https://celld.dev/): self-hosted platform context; supported APIs and qualifications evolve.
- [Amazon S3 conditional writes](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/AmazonS3/latest/userguide/conditional-writes.html) and [consistency model](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/AmazonS3/latest/userguide/Welcome.html): conditional creation/overwrite and strong read-after-write consistency are separate requirements. An arbitrary S3-compatible endpoint is not automatically qualified.

Guarantees remain conditional on storage semantics, clock assumptions where used, retained protocol data, and the documented failure model. A durability proof does not imply exactly-once application effects or atomic transactions across objects. Bucket service internals still bear coordination and replication costs.
