# Tab Into the Future

A browser that meets you halfway. Book 6 of *From RLCD to Predictive Tab*.

This series explains a proposed product grounded in the project charter. Page examples and work diagrams are illustrative; they are not measured performance results.

Source note: the narration is pinned to the September 16, 2026 source snapshot. References to the imported native implementation describe that snapshot; subsequent foundation work may have advanced.

### A suggestion becomes focus only when accepted

The proposed local assistant distinguishes a predicted-target halo from actual keyboard focus. In enabled contexts, Tab accepts the visible suggestion and moves focus; it does not activate the target or complete an autonomous task.

### Helpful assistance preserves a way through the page

Uncertainty and stale targets return control to ordinary navigation. Escape, reverse navigation, and application editing shortcuts retain their meaning. Site and context compatibility are part of the product's usefulness.

### Separate human feedback from the system's own echo

Showing a suggestion, moving focus, and seeing meaningful subsequent interaction are different events. Treating extension-produced focus as confirmation would manufacture a self-reinforcing training signal.

{% endviz %}

### Evaluate each new personal checkpoint

Acceptance alone can be ambiguous. Controlled feedback, retained provenance, separate recording and training controls, and rollback let personalization follow the person without requiring continuous background weight updates.

### Prove one useful workflow before broadening the scope

Start on a limited site with a small ranking baseline. Shadow predictions reveal errors before anything moves; visible suggestions and opt-in acceptance follow only when evidence supports them.

### The destination is useful work completed with less waiting

The checked autonomous runner and explicit hybrid mode extend the same foundation. The final gate is faster completed tasks at a predeclared reliability level, while predictive Tab remains a small user-controlled moment built on that evidence.
